Description · 50 wordsOpis · 50 riječi
The old order is not coming back. In The New Disorder, Domagoj Juričić argues that beneath war, great-power rivalry and technological change lies a deeper crisis: the loss of trust, legitimacy and shared meaning. Without rebuilding them inside societies, he warns, no stable international order is possible.Svijet se ne vraća u stari poredak. U knjizi The New Disorder Domagoj Juričić tvrdi da ispod ratova, rivalstva velikih sila i tehnoloških promjena leži dublja kriza: gubitak povjerenja, legitimiteta i zajedničkog smisla. Bez njihove obnove unutar društava, upozorava, nema ni stabilnog međunarodnog poretka.
Bio · one paragraphBiografija · odlomak
Domagoj Juričić has more than two decades of experience in diplomacy, public administration and international strategic advisory. He served as Chief of Staff to the President of Croatia, State Secretary in the Croatian Government and First Secretary at the Croatian Embassy in Washington. He advises on political and regulatory risk at MKPS, is a member of The New Club of Paris and the author of The New Disorder.Domagoj Juričić ima više od dva desetljeća iskustva u diplomaciji, državnoj upravi i međunarodnom strateškom savjetovanju. Bio je predstojnik Ureda predsjednice Republike Hrvatske, državni tajnik u Vladi i prvi tajnik u Veleposlanstvu RH u Washingtonu. Savjetuje o političkim i regulatornim rizicima u MKPS-u, član je mreže The New Club of Paris i autor knjige The New Disorder.
